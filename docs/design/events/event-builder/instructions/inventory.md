# Inventory

The Inventory category groups the instructions that change what the player owns or has achieved.

## Manage player items

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Manage player items lets users add or remove items in the player's inventory.

## Manage player money

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Manage player money lets users give money to the player or take money from them.

## Ideas

- **Manage Pokédex** could let users record which creatures the player has seen or caught.
- **Set active Pokédex** could let users choose which Pokédex the player uses, in a game that has several.
- **Give badge** could let users award the player a badge.
- **Manage creature data** could let users change the creatures the player owns, such as healing them or teaching them a move.
