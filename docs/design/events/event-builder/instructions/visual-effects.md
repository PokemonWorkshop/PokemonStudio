# Visual effects

The Visual effects category groups the instructions that act on the screen and on what appears on it.

## Change screen tone

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Change screen tone lets users tint the screen with a color, at once or gradually.

## Show animation

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Show animation lets users play an animation on the player, an event, or a position on the map.

## Show screen animation

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Show screen animation lets users animate the whole screen, such as making it shake or flash.

## Show emotion

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Show emotion lets users make an emotion appear above the player or an event, to convey how that character reacts.

## Manage image

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Manage image lets users place an image on the screen and change its position, size, or opacity.

## Manage camera

> 🚧 **This instruction is still being explored.**

Manage camera lets users move or zoom the camera, center it on a target, and reset it.

## Ideas

- **Manage dynamic light** could let users light up parts of a map.
