# Personas

Pokémon Studio users want to create their own Pokémon fan game. They fall into three main profiles:

- **Aspiring creators**: new to game creation and driven by enthusiasm, they need guidance to turn an idea into a finished game.
- **Game-making veterans**: power users who have already made games with RPG Maker XP, Pokémon Essentials, or another engine. They expect their experience and projects to carry over.
- **Programmers**: comfortable with Ruby, they adapt Pokémon SDK to their game and seek to go beyond what the interface offers.

Their expectations and technical knowledge differ widely: what feels obvious to a game-making veteran can overwhelm an aspiring creator. Pokémon Studio bridges this gap through [progressive complexity](principles.md#progressive-complexity).
